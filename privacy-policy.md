# Privacy Policy – MyCartify

MyCartify is a demo ecommerce mobile application created for educational and portfolio purposes.

## Information Collection and Use

MyCartify does not collect, store, or share any personal user information.

## Third-Party Services

The app uses a public API (DummyJSON) to display product data for demonstration purposes. No personal user data is transmitted or stored.

## Data Security

Since the application does not collect personal data, no user information is stored by the app.

## Children's Privacy

MyCartify is not directed toward children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

This privacy policy may be updated in the future. Any updates will be posted on this page.
